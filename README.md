# CDSL Equity Research

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **equity research and valuation analysis of Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL)**.

The project combines financial statement analysis, industry research, forecasting, valuation modelling, and investment analysis to evaluate CDSL's business performance and intrinsic value.

The analysis includes historical financial performance, future projections, DCF valuation, relative valuation, key financial assumptions, risk factors, and an overall investment research framework.

---

## Company

**Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL)**

CDSL is one of India's major securities depositories, providing electronic holding and settlement services for securities. Its business is closely linked with the growth of India's capital markets, demat accounts, investor participation, and financial market infrastructure.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

* Analyse CDSL's historical financial performance.
* Understand the company's business model and growth drivers.
* Study industry and capital-market trends affecting CDSL.
* Develop financial forecasts based on historical performance and reasonable assumptions.
* Estimate the company's intrinsic value using a **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)** approach.
* Perform relative valuation using **P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples**.
* Identify key business, financial, and market risks.
* Prepare an equity research report and supporting presentation.
* Develop a structured investment thesis based on quantitative and qualitative analysis.

## Valuation Methodology

The project uses multiple valuation approaches to assess CDSL's estimated fair value.

### Discounted Cash Flow

The DCF valuation estimates the present value of CDSL's future cash flows.

The major components include:

1. Forecast free cash flows
2. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
3. Terminal value
4. Present value of forecast cash flows
5. Present value of terminal value
6. Enterprise value
7. Equity value
8. Implied value per share

### Cost of Equity

The Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) was used:

```text
Ke = Rf + β × ERP
```

Key assumptions used in the model:

| Parameter           | Assumption |
| ------------------- | ---------: |
| Risk-free rate      |      6.91% |
| Beta                |       0.39 |
| Equity Risk Premium |      7.31% |
| Cost of Equity      |     ~9.76% |

The risk-free rate was based on the Indian 10-year government security benchmark, while beta and equity risk premium were sourced from market and valuation references used during the research process.

### Enterprise Value

The model uses:

```text
Enterprise Value = Market Capitalisation + Total Debt − Cash
```

The resulting enterprise value is used alongside the projected operating performance to evaluate CDSL's valuation.

## Relative Valuation

Alongside DCF, the project considers relative valuation methodologies:

* **P/E Multiple**
* **EV/EBITDA Multiple**

The valuation framework uses the following indicative weighting:

| Valuation Method | Weight |
| ---------------- | -----: |
| DCF              |    50% |
| P/E              |    30% |
| EV/EBITDA        |    20% |

This blended approach provides a broader perspective by combining intrinsic valuation with market-based valuation techniques.
